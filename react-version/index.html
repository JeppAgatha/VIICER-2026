What is i-BIS

An environmental intelligence platform, not a biorefinery

i-RIS is the hardware, a solar-powered biosensor stations placed directly in rivers. i-BIS is the software, a real-time contamination mapping, forecasting, and automated monitoring. Together, they replace slow, manual river testing with a system that never stops watching.

i-RIS hardware

Solar-powered biosensor stations that sit in the river and read pH, dissolved oxygen and heavy metals continuously, day and night, with no battery swaps.

i-BIS software

Contamination risk mapping, predictive forecasting, automated inspection scheduling and digital twin simulation, turning a stream of readings into a clear picture of river health.

i-RIS care

Remote monitoring and predictive maintenance that catch a failing sensor or a draining battery before it becomes a gap in the data.

Why it's different

Most water-quality tools track the basics: pH, dissolved oxygen, temperature. Heavy-metal contamination still tends to get caught by sending samples to a lab and waiting for results, not by reading it in the field as it happens. i-BIS closes that gap. It is the only system built for Southeast Asian rivers that combines real-time heavy-metal biosensing, AI-driven invasive-species detection and predictive maintenance delivered as an ongoing service, in one station, in the water.
